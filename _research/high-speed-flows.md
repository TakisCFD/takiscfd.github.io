## Research focus

Reliable simulation of high-speed flows requires numerical methods that remain robust near strong shocks while resolving the flow features relevant to aerodynamic and thermal predictions.

My work brings together compressible-flow algorithms, unstructured meshes and different levels of modelling fidelity. In the hypersonic regime, this includes the influence of coupled physical processes on the predicted solution.

## Questions that guide the work

- How sensitive are aerodynamic and aerothermal predictions to the numerical scheme and resolution?
- How should physical-model fidelity be matched to the flow regime and prediction of interest?
- Which verification and validation cases provide the most useful evidence for a simulation method?

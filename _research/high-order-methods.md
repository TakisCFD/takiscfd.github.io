## Research focus

High-order methods offer a route to resolving complex flow features efficiently, but their practical use depends on robustness, mesh flexibility and computational cost. My work develops finite-volume and discontinuous Galerkin approaches for unstructured meshes, including reconstruction and limiting strategies for flows with both smooth features and discontinuities.

## Questions that guide the work

- How can high-order accuracy be retained on mixed-element, geometrically complex meshes?
- How should numerical dissipation adapt to different flow features?
- How can robust shock capturing be combined with efficient treatment of smooth regions?

The methods are developed and assessed through verification problems and flow applications, with UCNS3D providing a platform for implementation and wider use.

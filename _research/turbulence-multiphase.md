## Research focus

Turbulent and multiphase flows bring together a wide range of spatial and temporal scales. My research examines how high-order discretisations and their dissipation properties influence the structures and interactions captured in a simulation.

Current themes include implicit large eddy simulation, adaptive adjustment of numerical dissipation and dispersion, and interface-capturing methods for compressible multicomponent systems.

## Connecting numerics and physics

- Understanding the effect of numerical choices on resolved turbulent structures.
- Assessing accuracy and robustness when shocks interact with material interfaces.
- Relating simulation results to appropriate verification and validation evidence.

Scientific visualisation helps communicate these interactions and provides a route into the underlying numerical and physical questions.

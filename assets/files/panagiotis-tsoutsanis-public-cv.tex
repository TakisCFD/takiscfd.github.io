\cvsection{Research overview}

My research focuses on the numerical methods that make complex fluid flows computable: accurate reconstruction on unstructured meshes, stable treatment of shocks and material interfaces, and efficient resolution of turbulence. I develop and analyse high-order finite-volume and discontinuous Galerkin methods, with particular attention to how numerical dissipation and dispersion affect the physics captured by a simulation.



This work connects algorithm development with applications in compressible, multiphase and high-speed flows. Through UCNS3D, I bring these methods into an open-source simulation framework and investigate how they can run efficiently on both CPUs and GPUs. The aim is to make accurate, robust CFD practical for complex geometries and large-scale calculations.



\cvsection{Areas of expertise}

\par\Needspace{4\baselineskip}{\bfseries High-order methods on unstructured meshes.} Finite-volume and discontinuous Galerkin formulations, accurate reconstruction and mixed-element meshes.\par\vspace{3pt}

\par\Needspace{4\baselineskip}{\bfseries Shock capturing and adaptive methods.} WENO and CWENO schemes, a posteriori MOOD limiting, and adaptive control of numerical dissipation and dispersion.\par\vspace{3pt}

\par\Needspace{4\baselineskip}{\bfseries Turbulence and aerodynamics.} Implicit large-eddy simulation, RANS modelling and the influence of numerical choices on aerodynamic predictions.\par\vspace{3pt}

\par\Needspace{4\baselineskip}{\bfseries Multiphase and multicomponent flows.} Diffuse-interface capturing, shock--interface interactions, cavitation and complex transient flows.\par\vspace{3pt}

\par\Needspace{4\baselineskip}{\bfseries High-speed and hypersonic flows.} Compressible-flow algorithms, shock--boundary-layer interactions and aerodynamic and thermal prediction.\par\vspace{3pt}

\par\Needspace{4\baselineskip}{\bfseries High-performance scientific computing.} Parallel algorithms, CPU and GPU execution, performance portability and open research software through UCNS3D.\par\vspace{3pt}

\cvsection{Teaching and supervision}

My teaching connects numerical analysis, fluid physics and scientific software. It covers numerical methods for compressible flows and partial differential equations, high-performance computing, turbulence modelling, mesh generation and simulation practice.



I am a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy and hold a postgraduate certificate in Academic Practice in Higher Education.

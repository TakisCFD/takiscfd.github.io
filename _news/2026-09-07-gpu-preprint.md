title: A common CPU and GPU implementation for UCNS3D
date: 2026-09-07
summary: A preprint describing OpenMP target acceleration of the high-order unstructured solver.
---
Our preprint, *Towards Heterogeneous Exascale CFD with a Single Fortran Code Base*, describes a single-source OpenMP target implementation for UCNS3D.

The work connects portable acceleration with verification of the numerical behaviour and evaluation of complete simulation workflows.

[Read the preprint on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07387).
